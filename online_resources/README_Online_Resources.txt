Online Resources for "Continual, Uncertainty-Aware and Structure-Consistent Federated Learning for Distributed Cyber-Physical Systems and Edge Digital Twins: A Critical Review, Unified Formulation and Reproducible Benchmark"
T. H. Sardar, Dayananda Sagar University (tanvir-cse@dsu.edu.in)

Online Resource 1  Screening
  OR1_screening_added_records.csv            the 73 records added from IEEE Xplore and Web of Science: decisions,
                                             exclusion reasons, labels, evidence levels and reference numbers
  OR1_kappa_tools.py                         draws a stratified reliability sample and computes Cohen's kappa
  (The 316 Scopus records are listed, with their database sources, in OR5_merged_records_671.csv.)

Online Resource 2  Claim-to-source tables
  OR2_claim_verification_fulltext.csv        every sentence or table row of the draft that cited one of the 166
                                             records read in full (476 claims), with the full-text verdict
                                             (supported / partly supported / contradicted / not found / list mention),
                                             the evidence and the correction made in the manuscript
  OR2_claim_to_source_added_and_new_references.csv
                                             claims citing added records, background references or pilot records,
                                             with their basis

Online Resource 3  Benchmark-characteristics extraction
  OR3_benchmark_extraction_added_records.csv abstract-level extraction for the 49 added primary records

Online Resource 4  Full-text extraction
  OR4_fulltext_extraction_166_records.csv    full-text extraction for the 166 cited records read in full: setting, data,
                                             clients, drift, communication and physical topology, graph data, uncertainty
                                             and calibration metrics, continual mechanism and metrics, structure-consistency
                                             test, costs, realism level, hardware and whether devices train, code release,
                                             guarantees, focal labels, key results, stated limitations and observations

Online Resource 5  Search and verification
  OR5_search_queries_all_databases.txt       database-specific query strings and search dates
  OR5_merge_search_exports.py                merging, de-duplication and field-equivalence test
  OR5_merged_records_671.csv                 the 671 unique records (316 Scopus, 355 from IEEE Xplore and Web of Science)
                                             with their sources and test result
  OR5_reference_verification_log.csv         all 260 references checked by DOI, title and year

Online Resource 6  Benchmark
  OR6_benchmark_code.zip                     code, run script, figure script, seeds and reference outputs of Section 10
                                             (the folder benchmark/ in https://github.com/TanvirProjs/fl-cps-review);
                                             see benchmark/README.md inside for instructions

All files are also available at https://github.com/TanvirProjs/fl-cps-review
