3) SCIENCEDIRECT   (sciencedirect.com > Advanced search > field
   "Title, abstract or author-specified keywords")
=====================================================================================
ScienceDirect allows at most 8 Boolean operators and no wildcards, so the query is split into
2 x 3 = 6 sub-queries. Plurals/hyphen variants are matched automatically.

SD-1  ("federated learning" OR "federated optimization") AND ("cyber-physical system" OR "industrial internet of things" OR IIoT) AND ("continual learning" OR "lifelong learning" OR "online learning" OR "concept drift")
SD-2  ("federated learning" OR "federated optimization") AND ("digital twin" OR "edge computing" OR "edge intelligence") AND ("continual learning" OR "lifelong learning" OR "online learning" OR "concept drift")
SD-3  ("federated learning" OR "federated optimization") AND ("cyber-physical system" OR "industrial internet of things" OR IIoT) AND ("uncertainty quantification" OR "uncertainty-aware" OR "predictive uncertainty" OR "uncertainty calibration")
SD-4  ("federated learning" OR "federated optimization") AND ("digital twin" OR "edge computing" OR "edge intelligence") AND ("uncertainty quantification" OR "uncertainty-aware" OR "predictive uncertainty" OR "uncertainty calibration")
SD-5  ("federated learning" OR "federated optimization") AND ("cyber-physical system" OR "industrial internet of things" OR IIoT) AND ("topology-aware" OR "topology-preserving" OR "network topology" OR "graph structure")
SD-6  ("federated learning" OR "federated optimization") AND ("digital twin" OR "edge computing" OR "edge intelligence") AND ("topology-aware" OR "topology-preserving" OR "network topology" OR "graph structure")

"federated machine learning" is dropped to stay within 8 operators; it is a phrase that
"federated learning" does not contain, so state this in the Methods (its effect is negligible
because nearly all such papers also say "federated learning").
Filters: Article type = Research articles, Review articles, Conference abstracts / proceedings.
Export: set 100 results per page > select all > Export > Export citation to RIS
        (repeat per page). Save as: sciencedirect_sd1.ris ... sciencedirect_sd6.ris
